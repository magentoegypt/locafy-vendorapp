Delete your Locafy Marketplace vendor account

This page explains how to delete your vendor account and associated data from Locafy Marketplace (shown on your device as Locafy Seller, package magentoegypt.locafy), published on Google Play by Clickalize Agency and operated by {{COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME}}.

Option 1 - delete your account in the app

  1. Open Locafy Seller and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and go to Profile.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Confirm when asked. You are signed out immediately and your vendor account is closed.

Option 2 - ask us to delete it

If you cannot sign in, or you no longer have the app installed, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the address registered to your vendor account, with:

  • the subject line Delete my Locafy Marketplace vendor account
  • your vendor/shop name
  • the email address and phone number on the account

We reply to confirm the request, and complete the deletion within 30 days. We may ask one question to verify you own the account before we act - we will never ask for your password.

What we delete

Deleting your account removes:

  • your vendor profile - name, email address, phone number, address and tax registration number;
  • your payout details, including bank name and branch;
  • your shop's product listings, images and descriptions;
  • your messages, support tickets and reviews you submitted;
  • your saved warehouse and store-pickup locations;
  • the push-notification token for your devices, so we stop contacting you.

Note to publisher - resolve before publishing this page. Confirm whether the backend removes the vendor record outright or deactivates it, and describe that here accurately. If accounts are deactivated and purged later, say so and give the delay.

What we keep, and why

Some records cannot be deleted on request, because we are legally required to keep them:

  • Completed orders, invoices, credit memos and payout records - kept for 5 years from the end of the financial year of the transaction, to meet tax, accounting and audit obligations. These stay linked to your shop's historical trading records, not to a usable account.
  • Anonymised or aggregated analytics - retained without anything that identifies you personally.
  • Records of the deletion request itself - kept so we can show the request was honoured.

Once the retention period ends, these records are deleted or anonymised.

How long it takes

  • In-app deletion: your account is closed immediately and you are signed out.
  • Email request: completed within 30 days of us verifying the request.
  • Backups: residual copies in encrypted backups are overwritten within 90 days.

Questions

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. This page covers the vendor app only; if you shop on Locafy as a customer, your customer account is separate and must be deleted separately.
